LOCO

Loco, whose real name is 권혁우

was born on 12월 25일(크리스마스!!), 1989, in 도곡동, Gangnam District, Seoul, 대한민국.

He is Korean and belongs to the Andong Kwon clan.

He is 174.6 cm tall, weighs 63 kg, and has blood type A. His mother’s name is Jung Hee-sook.

He has two pet dogs: Latte, a male Pomeranian mix born in 2016, and Heart, a female Chihuahua mix born in 2017.

For his education, he attended Seoul Hongneung Elementary School before transferring to Seoul Suam Elementary School, where he graduated.

He later graduated from Junggye Middle School and Jaehyun High School.

He studied economics at 홍익대학교(경제학과!!!!!).

Loco debuted in 2012 with the single “See The Light.”

He gained major recognition after winning the rap competition show “쇼미더머니.”

He completed his military service as a conscripted policeman in the Korean National Police Agency from February 7, 2019, to September 12, 2020.

He is signed under the label Locomotive and is also part of the crew VVD.

His fandom name is “Loggochu,” and his MBTI personality type is INFJ.
